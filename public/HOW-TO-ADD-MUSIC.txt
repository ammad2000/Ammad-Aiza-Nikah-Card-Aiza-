Background nasheed
==================

The card has a soft-music player with a mute/unmute button (bottom-right).
To enable the sound, add your chosen audio here:

  1. Pick a soft nasheed or instrumental you like.
  2. Save it as an MP3 named exactly:  nasheed.mp3
  3. Put that file in this folder:  walima-invitation/public/

That's it. When a guest taps "Open Invitation", the nasheed plays softly,
and the round button in the bottom-right corner mutes/unmutes it.

Tips:
- Keep it small (about 1-3 MB) so it loads fast on mobile data.
- Loop-friendly, gentle tracks work best.
- If you don't add a file, the card still works perfectly; it just stays silent.
